Hello! I’m Tianhao Wu, currently a Postdoctoral Researcher at the College of Computing and Data Science, Nanyang Technological University, Singapore, supervised by [Prof. Siqiang Luo](https://siqiangluo.com). 

My research lies at the intersection of graph algorithms and mathematical optimization, aiming to make large-scale reasoning and search more interpretable and computationally efficient. I’m particularly interested in graph representation learning and optimal transport. 

I obtained my Ph.D. in Mathematics from the Department of Mathematical Sciences, Tsinghua University, in 2025, under the supervision of [Prof. Zhongyi Huang](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=4cUP2_kAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao) and [Prof. Hao Wu](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=IvsMe-gAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao). Before that, I earned my Bachelor’s degree in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from the School of Mathematics, Tianjin University, in 2020, where I was advised by [Prof. Yong Zhang](https://cam.tju.edu.cn/homepage/zhangyong/). 

My broader research interests include graph similarity search, graph-based vector databases, and quantization methods. 

Outside of research, I enjoy reading, photography, hiking, and writing blogs. Feel free to reach out if you’d like to connect or collaborate! 

You can find my CV here: [English Version](/assets/CV_tianhao.pdf) and [Chinese Version](/assets/CV_tianhao_Chinese.pdf).
